### Data Visualization (BUS5VA)

Updated on Nov 2026.

La Trobe University - Semester 1, 2023.

This repository documents the learning journey for Visual Analytics (BUS5VA), a subject focused on the theory, application, and communication of data visualisation in business and analytical decision-making.

### Subject overview

As data becomes increasingly complex, visualisation is essential for helping managers and decision-makers interpret information, identify patterns, and communicate insights effectively. This subject introduces a range of visual analytics perspectives, including temporal, spatial, multivariate, text/document, and network-based visualisation, with applications in reporting, trend analysis, process monitoring, and real-time decision support.

### Learning outcomes

On successful completion of this subject, I have been able to:

- Interpret the importance of visualisation as a means to address information overload for managers and consumers of analytics.
- Inspect the visualisation framework in a variety of business problems.
- Evaluate the appropriate visualisation techniques for a variety of business problems.
- Apply the appropriate tools to visualise data and analytical results for each given user requirement.
- Appraise the various visualisation decisions across the entire solution process for a given problem.
- Tools learned: Tableau, SAS, Power BI, MS Excel.
- Frameworks applied: Bertin's Semiology, Mackinlay's Ranking, The Financial Times Visual Vocabulary, and Gestalt Principles.
